The mark

A compass gear. Direction and machine.

An orange ring holds four gear arcs on the diagonals around a four point compass rose. North is the one orange point. The rest of the rose and the gear take the ink, with light falling from the upper left.

The wordmark sets Traction in Mona Sans at weight 650 and width 110, and raises a small IT beside it in Fraunces Medium Italic. Use the files above every time; a retyped wordmark drifts within a week.

Type

Two families, two jobs.

Mona Sans 650, width 110

Headlines, section headings and the wordmark. Upright only.

We run your marketing and outreach.

Fraunces Regular

Optical size 14. Paragraphs, fields, anything read at length.

Fifteen minutes on a call starts the system: a shortlist, the openers, the outreach and the retention after the first win.

Fraunces Medium Italic

Eyebrows, buttons, nav links and badges, in sentence case.

Tired of chasing the wrong customers?

Mona Sans names or heads a thing. Fraunces carries anything a reader reads or presses, and the footer wordmark keeps the serif. Set every label in sentence case. Keep monospace capitals off every Traction surface.

Photography

Black and white, with one orange region.

Shoot real scenes: desks, calls, paper, night offices, streets, hands with phones. Push them to hard black and white, then recolour exactly one region. If a picture needs two orange targets to make sense, pick another picture.

Voice

Write like the person doing the work.

  1. 01

    Cut every em dash.

  2. 02

    Use the active voice and name the real actor.

  3. 03

    Make the reader the subject. Going imperative usually does it.

  4. 04

    Say what a thing is and stop.

  5. 05

    Drop hype, decorative modifiers and warm up openings.
