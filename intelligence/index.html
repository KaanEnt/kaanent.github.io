Frequently Asked Questions

What is PowerSell?+

Four agents that research a prospect, match your products to what they need, and write the outreach. You review the draft and send it.

Who is it for?+

Sales teams and founders who would rather talk to qualified prospects than research them.

How do the AI agents work?+

We give you four agents that work in parallel to:

  • Profile the customer and research them in depth
  • Analyze their pain points
  • Match your services to the right clients
  • Write personalized outreach messages
When will I get access?+

We onboard from the waitlist in small batches. Join it and we will email you when your spot opens.

What happens to my data?+

Research only ever touches public information. Results stay against your account and go nowhere else.
