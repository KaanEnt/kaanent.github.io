Privacy Policy

Updated October 21, 2026

Traction IT is a small consultancy in Toronto. This page says what we collect on this site, why, and where it goes. Short version: you give us an email when you want something from us, and we use it for that thing. With your permission, we also measure how you found us.

What we collect

You hand us information in four places on this site:

  • The brief form: your email and a sentence about your best customer.
  • The booking calendar: your name, your email, your time zone, the time you picked, and any notes you add.
  • The Traction Memo signup: your email.
  • The PowerSell waitlist: your email.

The build menu writes an email for you to send from your own inbox. We see it when it arrives, like any email you send us.

With analytics on, we also record how you use the site: the pages you open, the link that brought you here, and how far you get through the forms. The Cookie Policy lists every cookie and what each one records. When you click a link in an email we sent you, we record the click.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses and browser details, to run the site and block abuse.

How we use it

Everything we collect serves a purpose you can name:

  • Reply to your brief and prepare for the first call.
  • Book the call you picked and send you the invite.
  • Send you the Memo each week, if you asked for it.
  • Tell you when PowerSell opens, if you joined the waitlist.
  • Look up public information about you and your company, like your website, hiring posts, and funding news, so the first conversation is worth your time.
  • See which posts, emails, and pages bring people to us, and where the forms lose them.
  • Show the currency and the meeting times that fit where you are.

We never sell your information. We never rent lists.

Where it goes

Your information travels to the tools we use to run the business:

  • Resend delivers our email, including form notifications and the Memo.
  • Slack notifies us when a form comes in.
  • Cal.com schedules the calls you book and sends the calendar invite.
  • Supabase stores the waitlist in the United States, and our CRM and analytics records in Frankfurt, Germany. We build and run the CRM ourselves; it keeps a record of each person we talk to, with the emails and calls between us.
  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps its server logs.

Each provider processes your information to do a job for us and gets nothing to use for itself. Your information may be processed outside your country, including in Canada, the United States, and Germany. Where EU or UK law requires a safeguard for that transfer, we rely on the standard contractual clauses in each provider's data processing agreement.

Email, consent, and unsubscribing

You only get recurring email from us when you asked for it, through the Memo signup or the waitlist. Every message includes a working unsubscribe link. Use it and you are off the list within 10 business days, usually the same day. We follow Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation for everything we send.

Cookies and analytics

Two cookies measure how people find and use this site. In the EU, the UK, Switzerland, and Quebec, they wait until you turn analytics on. Everywhere else, you can turn them off with one click when you arrive. Either way, Cookie settings in the footer of every page changes your choice, and we treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a refusal.

We run no advertising cookies, and we never sell or share your information for advertising. The Cookie Policy lists every cookie, what it records, and how long it lasts.

How long we keep it

We keep briefs, bookings, and subscriber emails while the relationship is live. Analytics records expire 13 months after we make them. We keep a record of each cookie choice for 3 years, so we can show what you agreed to. Unsubscribe or ask us to delete your information and we remove it from our systems, minus what we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

Legal bases in the EU and the UK

If you are in the EU or the UK, we rely on these grounds:

  • Briefs and bookings: the steps you asked us to take before any agreement.
  • The Memo, the waitlist, and analytics: your consent. Withdraw it any time, and the withdrawal stops future processing.
  • Research on you and your company, recording clicks on links in our email, and server logs: our legitimate interest in running a sales business and keeping the site secure. Object to any of it and we stop, unless a compelling reason outweighs your interest.

Your rights

Ask us any time what we hold about you, and we will show you, correct it, delete it, or send you a copy. Write to contact@traction.it.com. We answer within 30 days.

We handle personal information under PIPEDA, Canada's federal privacy law, and Quebec's Law 25. If you are unhappy with how we responded, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or in Quebec to the Commission d'accès à l'information.

In the EU and the UK, you can also restrict or object to how we use your information, and complain to your data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK. In California and the other US states with privacy laws, you have the same rights to know, correct, and delete, and using them never changes how we treat you.

Who is responsible

Kaan Un, founder, is responsible for personal information at Traction IT, including as the person in charge of the protection of personal information under Quebec's Law 25. Reach Kaan at contact@traction.it.com.

Children

This site is for people running or buying for a business. It is not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

When our practices change, this page changes with them, and the date at the top moves. When a change adds a cookie or a new use for one, we ask for your choice again. Big changes to how we use email get announced in the email itself.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to contact@traction.it.com. Traction IT operates from Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
