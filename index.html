What Traction Does

From your best customer to the next conversations.

Four stages. One brief. Fifteen minutes on a call, then Traction runs the system.

  1. 01 / Leads

    We find them.

    Public signals first. New locations, manufacturer lines, hiring posts, dated events. Specific accounts shaped after your best customer.

  2. 02 / Qualifying

    We qualify them.

    Within fifteen minutes of the first call, a shortlist with the why, the signal, and the first opener. Then the system keeps moving.

  3. 03 / Outreach

    We send the first message.

    Each opener written off the signal that surfaced the account, in your voice, and sent from your inbox.

  4. 04 / Retention

    We help you keep them.

    We set up retention so first customers become repeat ones. Renewal triggers, expansion signals, and the next quiet move before churn appears.
