Volume is dead.

Gmail, Yahoo, and Outlook now treat 5,000 emails a day to their consumer inboxes as bulk. No SPF + DKIM + DMARC means an SMTP reject. That is the official rule. Copy never gets a vote.

Practitioners still cap around 30 sends per inbox per day. Instantly wrote that in their July 2026 ops guide. You scale with more inboxes, not more volume through one.

LinkedIn is not the unlock. The working ceiling is about 100 connection requests a week. Sales Navigator is 50 InMails a month. You cannot buy more invites.

Then the work is the list. Enrich the shortlist. Stop on the first valid email. Instantly's 2026 benchmark: average reply 3.43%. Elite is 10.7%+. The gap is not more mailboxes. It is who you send to.

After the first win, outbound the people who already bought. A new hire on the account. A champion who left. Not a cold sequence to your own customers.

The image is the path. Authenticate. Cap. Invite. Waterfall, then keep.
